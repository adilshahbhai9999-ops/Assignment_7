Chicken Biryani

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Chicken
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Yogurt
  • Biryani Masala
  • Oil

Instructions

  1. Wash and boil the rice.
  2. Cook chicken with spices.
  3. Add onions, tomatoes and yogurt.
  4. Mix chicken and rice in a pot.
  5. Cook on low heat for 15 minutes.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy.
